# LeetCode Placement Roadmap — Must-Do Problems by Pattern

**Source:** Cross-referenced Striver A2Z Sheet, NeetCode 150, Love Babbar 450, r/developersIndia  
**Target:** Indian campus placements (TCS Digital, Infosys, Wipro, Capgemini, Zoho, Samsung)  
**Created:** 2026-10-02

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Problems marked with ★ appear across multiple lists AND are frequently asked in interviews. Do these first.

---

## Realistic Targets

- **Quality over quantity:** 150-200 well-understood problems > 500 copy-pasted ones
- **3-5 problems/week** consistently = 150+ by placement season
- **Pattern recognition** matters more than problem count
- **Aptitude clears the gate** — if you fail apti, LeetCode skills are useless
